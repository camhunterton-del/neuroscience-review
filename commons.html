The Commons

The Commons.

A shared space for the people who love this stuff. Short notes from the field. What you're working on, what you saw at a conference, the question you can't stop thinking about.

The essays here are careful, edited, and slow. The Commons is the opposite. It's a place for the quick stuff, a paragraph or two from someone in or around neuroscience, passing along something worth knowing. A talk that stuck with you. A method you're excited about. A finding you're still chewing on.

Anyone in the field or learning it can take part, students included. Every note is read and approved before it goes up, so the bar isn't your credentials. It's that the note is real, clear, and worth a reader's minute.

What a note looks like

Short. A title, a few sentences, your name and where you are. No sources to format, no essay to write, no code to touch. If you can explain it to a curious friend in a paragraph, it belongs here.

How to take part

Email your note to theneuroreview@gmail.com with the subject "Commons," and one line about who you are. We read every one, and if it's a fit, it goes up with your name on it. That's the whole process.

Notes are moderated before they appear. By sending one you agree to our submission terms.
