Everything on this site is published for education and general interest. It explains what research has found and what it means. It is not medical advice, and it is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan for you or anyone else.

This is not a substitute for professional care

The brain, mental health, medications, and disease are exactly the topics where individual details matter. What is true across a population may be wrong for one person. Nothing you read here should replace a conversation with a qualified doctor, therapist, pharmacist, or other licensed professional who knows your situation.

Always seek the advice of a qualified professional with any questions about a medical or mental health condition. Never disregard professional advice, or delay getting it, because of something you read on this site. Reading The Neuroscience Review does not create a doctor and patient relationship of any kind.

If this is an emergency

If you think you are having a medical or mental health emergency, do not use this site. Call your local emergency number right away. In the United States you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, or call 911.

We check every piece against primary sources before it goes out, and we try hard to represent the science honestly. We do not promise that everything here is complete or current, and we are not responsible for decisions made based on it. If you spot an error, please tell us at theneuroreview@gmail.com.
