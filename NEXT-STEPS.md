# The Neuroscience Review — Next Steps

Site is live at https://camhunterton-del.github.io/neuroscience-review/ with the design and sharing upgrades in place (Open Graph link previews, RSS, dark mode, bylines, share buttons, key-takeaways, sources blocks, reading typography). The build is done. From here the game is **audience and cadence**, not more code.

Priorities, highest leverage first.

## 1. Turn on email capture (you, ~2 min)
Create a free Buttondown account and send the username. The form on all 4 pages is already wired to Buttondown with a graceful fallback, so the moment you give the username, signups go live. Until then, every reader who would have subscribed is lost. This is the single highest-value unblock.

## 2. Publish post 3 and hold a weekly cadence
Consistency is what compounds for a blog. Strong next candidates from the roadmap: how memory actually works, a recent psychedelics-and-depression trial, or the blood-brain barrier. Claude can draft the next one in your voice and to your editorial standards, and you fact-check before it goes up. Aim for one post a week.

## 3. Custom domain (~$10/yr)
Something like theneurosciencereview.com reads as more credible and is far more shareable than the github.io link. Claude can walk you through buying it (Porkbun or Cloudflare) and point it at the site.

## 4. Per-post share images
Right now there is one branded share image for the whole site. A unique 1200x630 image per article makes links pop when shared. Claude supplies the ChatGPT prompts, you generate, Claude drops them in and wires the meta tags.

## 5. Distribution (once 3 to 4 posts are up)
Cross-post to relevant communities, submit to a couple of science-writing aggregators, and cross-promote with Stethos, which shares the same premed and neuro audience. The foundational neuron post is good link bait for other writers.

## 6. Light, privacy-friendly analytics
A simple counter so you can see which posts land, without ads or invasive tracking. Helps decide what to write next.
