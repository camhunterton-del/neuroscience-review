Audrey Drotos

Auditory neuroscientist and guest contributor

Audrey Drotos is an auditory neuroscientist who studies how the brain makes sense of sound. She is writing her first piece for The Neuroscience Review on how the auditory system turns vibrations in the air into the experience of hearing, from the pressure waves that reach your ear to the moment they become speech, music, and meaning.

Her byline and full piece will appear here when it publishes.
