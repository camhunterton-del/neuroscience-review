We're walking to end Alzheimer's

Friday, November 14, 2026 · Central Park, New York City

We're raising $10,000 for the Alzheimer's Association. Walk with us or chip in whatever you can. Every dollar goes straight to the fight.

Donate Walk with us

Why we're walking

We write about Alzheimer's a lot at The Neuroscience Review, the new drugs, the risk factors, the science that's still being worked out. This fall we're doing something about it. On November 14 we're walking in Central Park with the Alzheimer's Association, and we'd love for you to be part of it.

You can walk with the team or give whatever you're able to. Both help fund the research, care, and support that families living with this disease depend on.

  • Every dollar goes to the AssociationDonations are made directly to the Alzheimer's Association through our team page. The Neuroscience Review never holds or handles the money.
  • Your gift is tax-deductibleThe Alzheimer's Association is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
  • Joining is freeRegistering to walk with the team costs nothing. Come spend a morning in Central Park with us.
